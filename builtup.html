PART 03 · DRIVER (1)

Built-up expansion — the foundation of the spreading high-heat zone

The Built Area class of the Esri Sentinel-2 Land Cover Explorer (10 m resolution) is used to map Hanoi's built-up land in 2017 and 2025, and the area that changed between the two dates. Concrete, roofs and roads absorb and hold more heat than bare soil, water and vegetation. Built-up expansion is therefore a direct driver of the spreading high land surface temperature zone.

    CONCLUSION

    Built-up land is densest in the city center and along both banks of the Red River. Expansion appears mostly on the periphery, surrounding and connecting existing built-up patches, showing urbanization spreading from the urban core outward, in the same direction as the high-heat zone in Part 02. The western and northern fringes remain mostly non-built-up. As concrete replaces bare land and greenery, what happens to vegetation? Part 04 answers.
