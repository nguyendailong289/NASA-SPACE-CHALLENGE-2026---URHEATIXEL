PART 02 · TREND

High land surface temperature zones are spreading from the urban core to the periphery

Annual mean LST (°C) is built from the daily MODIS MOD11A1 images. To compare two years with different overall temperatures, LST is min–max normalized to 0–1 within each year, then split into 5 classes in steps of 0.2: Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High.

CONCLUSION

The high-heat zone (High + Very High) grew from 21.1% to 65.8% of the area, mostly through Moderate → High (43.5% of the area). The zone also moved away from the center: its mean distance to Hoan Kiem Lake rose from 20.0 km to 26.8 km, and the share within 10 km fell from 18.8% to 9.4%. Because the data are normalized within each year, this is a change in spatial distribution, not in absolute temperature. So what is pushing the high-heat zone outward?
