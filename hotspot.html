    Key findings

      CONCLUSION

      Putting the story together: the spreading high-heat zone (Part 02) follows the same direction as built-up expansion (Part 03) and vegetation loss (Part 04). The inverse relationship between temperature and greenery is getting stronger (r from −0.24 to −0.42). Hotspots grew 52% to 192.4 km², concentrated in inner-city wards such as Hà Đông, Khương Đình, Tương Mai, Phương Liệt and Thanh Xuân; half of them were already hotspots in 2017. These are the places to intervene first. Priority levels are based on LST and NDVI normalized within each year, so they reflect each pixel's relative position within the city.
